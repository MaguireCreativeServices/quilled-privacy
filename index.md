# Quilled Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 16 November 2026**

## 1. Introduction

Quilled is a creative writing application for macOS and iOS, developed by Maguire Creative Services.

We respect your privacy and recognise that your manuscripts, stories, notes and creative work are personal and valuable.

This Privacy Policy explains how Quilled handles information when you use the application, including its optional integration with Dropbox.

## 2. Information Quilled Handles

Quilled may process information you create or import into the application, including:

- Manuscripts, chapters and writing projects.
- Story notes, character information and other writing resources.
- Application preferences and writing progress.
- Files and related information needed for backup, restoration or synchronisation features.

Quilled does not require you to publish or share your writing to use its core writing features.

## 3. Storage of Your Writing

Quilled stores writing data on your device and may use storage or synchronisation services that you choose to enable.

Your manuscripts remain your intellectual property. Using Quilled does not transfer ownership of your writing to Maguire Creative Services.

## 4. Dropbox Integration and Synchronisation

Quilled uses Dropbox to provide optional cloud storage and synchronisation of writing projects between devices.

When you connect your Dropbox account, Quilled may:

- Save your manuscripts, chapters, notes and associated project data to Dropbox.
- Synchronise changes between your Mac, iPhone and other supported devices.
- Access and update previously saved project files to keep your writing consistent across devices.
- Retrieve stored writing data when you reconnect or use Quilled on another device.
- Synchronise regularly while the integration is enabled.

Dropbox access is authorised by you and is used to provide these features.

Your writing remains your intellectual property. Maguire Creative Services does not claim ownership of your manuscripts.

You can disconnect Quilled from Dropbox through your Dropbox account settings. Disconnecting does not automatically delete files already stored in Dropbox.

Files stored in Dropbox are also subject to [Dropbox's Privacy Policy](https://www.dropbox.com/privacy).

## 5. Sharing of Information

Maguire Creative Services does not sell your manuscripts or personal information to advertisers.

Information may be processed by third-party services, such as Dropbox, when you choose to use those services.

## 6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to design Quilled with the privacy and security of your writing in mind.

However, no application, device or online storage service can guarantee absolute security.

You are encouraged to maintain independent backups of important writing projects.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Writing projects stored locally can be managed through the application's available features.

Files stored in connected third-party services may need to be managed or deleted through those services.

Disconnecting Dropbox revokes future access through that authorisation but does not necessarily delete files already stored in Dropbox.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Quilled is a general-purpose creative writing application and is not specifically directed towards children under 13.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Quilled develops or its features change.

Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.
